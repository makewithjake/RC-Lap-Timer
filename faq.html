Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about pricing, privacy, battery impact, roadmap, and where to send feature ideas.

FAQ List

What does it cost?

LapTrack is free.

Are there ads?

No ads.

Is an email required?

No. Privacy first — we don't collect or store any information.

How many cars can I track at the same time?

Just one for now. LapTrack is best for time trials, solo practice sessions, and rally runs.

Do you plan to add multi-car tracking?

Yes — it's in the pipeline. Keep an eye on laptrack.app for updates, or join our email list once available.

Will this app kill my phone battery?

No. Right now, power usage is about as much as scrolling TikTok.

I have an idea for a new feature. Where do I send it?

Email us at jake@makewithjake.net or submit an issue on GitHub.

Is this app open source?

Yes. If you want to help make it awesome, submit ideas or code via GitHub.
